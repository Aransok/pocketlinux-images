Mint-Y theme and icon attribution
=================================

This image contains the Mint-Y GTK theme family and the Mint-Y icon theme
family, built from the upstream source repositories at image build time:

  https://github.com/linuxmint/mint-themes
  https://github.com/linuxmint/mint-y-icons

Exact source revisions are listed in SOURCE-REVISIONS.txt next to this file.
The upstream copyright files are kept here as mint-themes.copyright and
mint-y-icons.copyright.
